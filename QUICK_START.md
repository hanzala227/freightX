## Quick Actions
- **Search**: Just type in top-right search box
- **Filter**: Click "Filter" button, then type in column inputs
- **Select All**: Click checkbox in header
- **Select Row**: Click anywhere on the row
- **Edit**: Click "VIEW" or File No
- **Quick View**: Click eye icon next to MBL No
- **Change Color**: Click colored square
- **Lock/Unlock**: Click lock icon
- **Export**: Click "Excel" button
